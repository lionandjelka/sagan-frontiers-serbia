WHY CARL SAGAN?

A scientific legacy built around the biggest questions.

Sagan Frontiers takes inspiration from Carl Sagan’s way of linking rigorous planetary science with the human questions raised by exploration: Where are we? Are we alone? How do we recognize intelligence? What responsibilities follow from the cosmic perspective?

Carl Sagan was an astronomer, planetary scientist, author and science communicator whose work connected Solar System exploration, the origins and possibility of life, SETI, critical thinking and public engagement with science. His legacy at Cornell continues through research on worlds beyond the Solar System and the search for life in the universe.

STRATEGIC ALIGNMENT

Connecting Serbia to Europe and Artemis.

SAGAN FRONTIERS is conceived as a platform for connection rather than isolation: a place to identify credible, visible and collaborative pathways for Serbian participation in European research networks and the international scientific ecosystem developing around Artemis.

ARTEMIS

Participation through scientific contribution

Planetary and lunar research, astrobiology, space agriculture, data analysis, enabling technologies, education, open science and responsible exploration.

EUROPE

European and university partnerships

Joint projects, co-supervision, student and staff mobility, research visits, shared training, consortium building and access to scientific infrastructures.

LIFE SUPPORT

Space agriculture

A bridge between sustainability, life sciences and Artemis-era exploration through food production, resource recycling, radiation, reduced gravity and closed ecological systems.

DATA

AI and data-enabled space science

Rubin/LSST, Gaia, astroinformatics, machine learning and advanced statistics as routes to global scientific contribution without requiring national launch capability.
